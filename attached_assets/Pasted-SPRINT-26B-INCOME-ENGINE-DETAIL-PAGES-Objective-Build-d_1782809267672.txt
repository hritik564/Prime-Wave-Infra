SPRINT 26B — INCOME ENGINE DETAIL PAGES

Objective

Build dedicated detail pages for every income engine.

These pages replace the old Financial Transparency experience.

Each page must answer:

1. Am I qualified?
2. When is my next payout?
3. How much am I expected to receive?
4. How do I increase this income?
5. What have I earned historically?

Do NOT expose implementation details before answering these questions.

This sprint is presentation + aggregation only.

Do NOT modify:

- ROI Engine
- Binary Engine
- Orbit Engine
- Salary Engine
- Level Income Engine
- Wallet Engine
- Investment Engine
- Deposit Verification
- Withdrawal
- Scheduler
- Database schema
- Financial calculations

Use existing production business logic only.

====================================================

SECTION 5

Short.

3-5 questions.

"Why is my payout lower?"

Answers should reuse actual business rules.
