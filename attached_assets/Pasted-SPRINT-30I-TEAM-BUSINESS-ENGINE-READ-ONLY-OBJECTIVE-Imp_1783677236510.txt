SPRINT 30I — TEAM BUSINESS ENGINE (READ-ONLY)

OBJECTIVE

Implement Team Business as a reporting feature ONLY.

This sprint must NOT modify any financial engine.

==========================================================
BUSINESS RULE
==========================================================

Team Business =

The cumulative APPROVED deposit volume of every member in the binary downline.

This includes:

• Normal user deposits
• Genesis funding deposits

Genesis funding is REAL business volume for reporting purposes.

It MUST contribute to Team Business.

==========================================================
IMPORTANT
==========================================================

Team Business is NOT a compensation metric.

It must NEVER generate:

• Binary Income
• Binary Orbit Income
• Salary
• Direct Income
• Level Income
• ROI
• BV
• Carry Forward

The Team Business engine is READ-ONLY.

==========================================================
SOURCE OF TRUTH
==========================================================

Use ONLY:

DepositRequest

WHERE

status = 'APPROVED'

Do NOT use:

Investment.amount

Wallet.depositWallet

WalletTransaction

leftBV

rightBV

==========================================================
TREE
==========================================================

Use ONLY the binary tree.

Traverse using:

binaryParentId

Do NOT use sponsorId.

==========================================================
CALCULATIONS
==========================================================

For every logged-in member calculate:

Left Team Business

Right Team Business

Total Team Business

Definitions:

Left Team Business

=

SUM(DepositRequest.amount)

for every APPROVED deposit inside the LEFT binary subtree.

Right Team Business

=

SUM(DepositRequest.amount)

for every APPROVED deposit inside the RIGHT binary subtree.

Total Team Business

=

Left + Right

==========================================================

They are REPORTING volume only.
