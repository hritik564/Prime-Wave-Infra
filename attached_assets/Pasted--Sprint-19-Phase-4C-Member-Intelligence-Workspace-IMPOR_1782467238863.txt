# Sprint 19 — Phase 4C

## Member Intelligence Workspace

IMPORTANT

This sprint upgrades the existing Member Drawer into a complete Member Intelligence Workspace.

DO NOT redesign the Members page.

DO NOT redesign the table.

DO NOT redesign the dashboard.

DO NOT modify routing.

DO NOT connect backend.

Use mock data only.

Everything should extend the existing Member Drawer.

---

## OBJECTIVE

When an admin opens a member,

they should immediately understand:

• Who this member is
• Their financial status
• Their network position
• Their recent activity
• Any warnings
• Any actions that require attention

without opening another page.
