# Sprint 19 — Phase 6

## Wallet Control Center

IMPORTANT

This module is the financial command center of Prime Wave.

It must provide administrators with complete visibility into every wallet across the platform.

This is NOT a banking screen.

This is an operations dashboard.

Use realistic mock data only.

Do NOT connect APIs.

Do NOT connect the database.

Do NOT modify existing modules.

Continue using the Prime Wave Admin design system.
