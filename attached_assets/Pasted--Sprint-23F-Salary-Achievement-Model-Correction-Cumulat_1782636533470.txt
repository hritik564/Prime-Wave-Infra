# Business Model Correction

Sprint 23E currently follows:

**Replacement Rank Model**

Example:

Bronze → OFF

Silver → ON

Gold → ON

This is NOT the Prime Wave business rule.

Replace it with:

**Cumulative Milestone Model**

Every salary milestone is earned independently.

Once earned, that milestone continues paying for its own duration.

Earlier milestones are NEVER deactivated simply because a higher milestone is achieved.

# Salary Achievement Rules

Each salary rank may only be earned ONCE.

Never create duplicate achievements for the same rank.

A member MAY have multiple active SalaryAchievements simultaneously.

Example:

Bronze

Silver

Gold

is VALID.

Example:

Bronze

Bronze

is NOT valid.

---

# Promotion Behaviour

Do NOT deactivate previously earned salary achievements.

Example:

Member qualifies for Bronze.

Create Bronze achievement.

Later qualifies for Silver.

Keep Bronze ACTIVE.

Create Silver achievement.

Later qualifies for Gold.

Keep Bronze ACTIVE.

Keep Silver ACTIVE.

Create Gold achievement.

Each achievement pays independently until its own duration expires.

---

# Monthly Salary Scheduler

Do NOT change scheduler timing.

The scheduler should:

Find every ACTIVE SalaryAchievement.

For each achievement:

Verify:

* Achievement still active
* Duration not completed
* Member still satisfies ACTIVE member rule

Then credit ONE monthly payment for THAT achievement.

Example:

Bronze = $40

Silver = $75

Monthly credit:

$115

NOT

$75
