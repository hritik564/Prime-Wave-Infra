# Milestone 3 — Network Intelligence

## Objective

Build a premium **Network Intelligence Center** that helps users understand, grow and manage their organization.

This is NOT simply a binary tree.

The page must provide insights first, visualization second.

The user should immediately understand:

* How their network is growing
* Which leg needs attention
* Where the next Binary Income is likely to come from
* Who is producing business
* Which members require follow-up
* How close they are to the next reward

Use existing production APIs.

Do not duplicate business logic.
