# Hotfix — Income Wallet Transfer Fee (15%) & 72-Hour Release Engine

Objective

Fix the Income Wallet transfer engine so it follows Prime Wave's locked business rules.

This is a financial logic fix.

Do NOT redesign the UI.

Do NOT modify wallet architecture.

Do NOT modify the 72-hour release engine except where required to apply the correct transfer amount.

--------------------------------------------------------
Business Rule (LOCKED)
--------------------------------------------------------

When a user transfers funds from the Income Wallet to the Withdrawal Wallet:

1. Deduct a ONE-TIME transfer fee of 15%.

2. The remaining 85% enters the 72-hour release engine.

3. The transfer fee must NEVER be deducted again during hourly releases.

Example:

User transfers:

$10.00

Transfer Fee (15%)

$1.50

Net Transfer

$8.50

72-Hour Engine

$8.50 ÷ 72

=

$0.118055... released every hour

After 72 releases:

Total Released = $8.50

Total Fee = $1.50

--------------------------------------------------------
Transfer Record
--------------------------------------------------------

Every transfer must store:

• Original Transfer Amount

• Transfer Fee (15%)

• Net Transfer Amount

• Hourly Release Amount

• Released Amount

• Remaining Amount

• Current Hour

• Status

Do not calculate the fee repeatedly.

Persist these values when the transfer is created.

Wallet Behaviour
--------------------------------------------------------

Immediately after transfer creation:

Income Wallet

↓

Deduct Original Transfer Amount

Transfer Queue

↓

Store Net Transfer Amount

Withdrawal Wallet

↓

Receive ONLY hourly releases from the Net Transfer Amount

The fee must never enter the Withdrawal Wallet.

--------------------------------------------------------
Validation
--------------------------------------------------------

Verify:

✓ 15% fee deducted exactly once.

✓ Only 85% enters the release engine.

✓ Hourly release amount is calculated from the net amount.

✓ Total released after 72 hours equals exactly the net transfer amount.

✓ No double deduction occurs.

✓ Progress calculations remain correct.

✓ Transfer history displays fee correctly.

✓ Wallet balances remain synchronized.

Goal

Ensure the Income Wallet transfer engine correctly deducts the one-time 15% transfer fee before starting the 72-hour release process, while maintaining complete financial accuracy and transparency.
