Sprint 31I — Member Financial Timeline (Admin UX Enhancement)

Objective

The Recent Transactions section inside the Member Drawer currently displays raw wallet transaction data, making it difficult for administrators to investigate income-related support tickets.

Convert the Recent Transactions section into a clean, human-readable financial timeline that explains every transaction from a business perspective.

This is a UI/UX enhancement only.

DO NOT change any business logic.

DO NOT modify any calculations.

DO NOT change any database schema.

DO NOT alter any ledger or wallet records.

====================================================

====================================================
Acceptance Criteria
====================================================

✓ No raw enum names are visible to administrators.

✓ Every transaction clearly answers:

• What happened?

• Why did it happen?

• Who caused it?

• How was the amount calculated?

• What wallet balance changed?

✓ Transactions are easy to audit without checking database records.

✓ No business logic changes.

✓ No financial calculation changes.

✓ No database schema changes.

✓ No impact on existing APIs or wallet engine.

This sprint is purely an Admin UX enhancement designed to make financial investigations significantly faster and easier.
