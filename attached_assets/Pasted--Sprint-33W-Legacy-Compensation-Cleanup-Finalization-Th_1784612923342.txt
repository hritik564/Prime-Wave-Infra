# Sprint 33W – Legacy Compensation Cleanup & Finalization

This sprint permanently removes every obsolete salary execution path that was superseded by the new SalaryRun architecture.

No new features.

No business logic changes.

No database redesign.

The objective is to eliminate deprecated code, simplify the codebase, and ensure there is only ONE canonical compensation engine.
