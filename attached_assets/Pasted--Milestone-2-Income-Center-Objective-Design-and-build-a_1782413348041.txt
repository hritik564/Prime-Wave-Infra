# Milestone 2 — Income Center

## Objective

Design and build a premium **Income Center** that becomes the single source of truth for every earning inside Prime Wave.

This replaces:

* Binary Income
* Binary Orbit
* Level Income
* Salary Income
* ROI History

There must NOT be separate pages for these anymore.

Every income source should flow into one unified premium financial experience.

This page should feel closer to Revolut, Binance, Stripe or Wise than a traditional MLM application.

Reuse existing backend services and APIs.

Do not duplicate business logic.
