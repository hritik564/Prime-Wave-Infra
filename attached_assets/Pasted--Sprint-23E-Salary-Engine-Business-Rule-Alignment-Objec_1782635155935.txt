--------------------------------------------------------
2. Qualification Uses BUSINESS ($)
--------------------------------------------------------

The first column represents TOTAL BUSINESS IN USD.

It does NOT represent BV.

Salary qualification must be calculated from total qualifying investment business in dollars.

Any logic currently treating these values as BV must be corrected.

--------------------------------------------------------
3. Bronze / Silver / Gold
--------------------------------------------------------

Qualification:

• Global ACTIVE member

• Required Total Business

No 40/60 leg split required.

Business can come from either leg.

--------------------------------------------------------
4. Platinum and Above
--------------------------------------------------------

Qualification:

• Global ACTIVE member

• Required Total Business

• 40% / 60% Power Leg / Weaker Leg split

--------------------------------------------------------
5. Immediate Qualification
--------------------------------------------------------

Salary qualification is EVENT DRIVEN.

Whenever qualifying business increases (investment or qualifying business event):

Immediately evaluate salary qualification.

If the member qualifies for a higher rank:

• Update Current Salary Rank immediately.

• Create ONE new SalaryAchievement.

• Store:

  - Rank
  - Qualified Date
  - Monthly Salary
  - Duration
  - End Date
  - Business Snapshot
  - Qualification Snapshot

• Send qualification notification.

Do NOT wait for the monthly salary scheduler.

--------------------------------------------------------
6. Highest Rank Only
--------------------------------------------------------

A member can have ONLY ONE active Salary Rank.

If multiple ranks are satisfied during the same evaluation:

Example:

Business = $12,700

Bronze ✔

Silver ✔

Gold ✔

Result:

Award ONLY Gold.

Do NOT create Bronze or Silver achievements.

If a higher rank is achieved later:

Deactivate the previous SalaryAchievement.

Create the new higher SalaryAchievement.

Never allow two active SalaryAchievements simultaneously.

--------------------------------------------------------
7. Monthly Salary Payments
--------------------------------------------------------

Monthly salary payments remain scheduler-driven.

The scheduler should:

Find active SalaryAchievements.

Verify:

• Achievement still active

• Payment duration not completed

• Member still satisfies the global ACTIVE member rule

Then:

Credit ONE monthly salary payment.

Salary is NOT paid immediately upon qualification.

Qualification is immediate.

Monthly payment remains monthly.

--------------------------------------------------------
