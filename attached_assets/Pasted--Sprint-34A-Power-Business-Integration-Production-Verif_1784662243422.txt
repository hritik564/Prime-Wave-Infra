# Sprint 34A — Power Business Integration & Production Verification

Sprint Goal

Power Business should behave exactly like normal Team Business throughout the platform except for ONE rule:

Power Business MUST NEVER generate Direct Referral Income or Level Income.

For all other business calculations, it should behave exactly like organic business.

This sprint must verify production behaviour and align the UI.

Do NOT change any investment, payout or compensation percentages.

--------------------------------------------------
BUSINESS RULES
--------------------------------------------------

Power Business is virtual business assigned by Super Admin.

Once assigned, it should behave exactly like normal Team Business.

Power Business MUST contribute to:

✓ Team Business

✓ Left Team Business

✓ Right Team Business

✓ Binary Matching

✓ Binary Orbit

✓ Salary Qualification

✓ Rank Qualification

✓ Business Progress

✓ Matching Calculations

✓ BV Flush

✓ Carry Forward

Power Business MUST NOT contribute to:

✗ Direct Referral Income

✗ Level Income

Direct and Level Income must always be generated only from real investments.

--------------------------------------------------
PART 1 — Production Verification
--------------------------------------------------

Using Mahakal (or another suitable production member):

Current example:

Organic Left BV = 0

Organic Right BV = 5

Assign:

Left Power BV = 200

Run one Binary Matching cycle.

Verify:

✓ Team Business immediately increases.

✓ Left Team Business increases.

✓ Matching candidate generated.

✓ computeBVFlush() executes.

✓ Matched BV = 5.

✓ Organic Right BV becomes 0.

✓ Power Left BV becomes 195.

✓ Matching Income credited.

✓ Wallet updated.

✓ Binary Orbit updated correctly.

✓ Salary business updated correctly.

Return database evidence.

--------------------------------------------------
PART 2 — Team Business Integration
--------------------------------------------------

Power Business must be included inside Team Business.

Update all Team Business calculations so that:

Team Business = Organic Business + Power Business

Update every page where Team Business is displayed.

Examples include (not limited to):

• Member Dashboard

• Admin User Details

• Network Tree

• Business Summary

• Rank Progress

• Binary Pages

• Team Statistics

Ensure every Team Business number is consistent throughout the application.

Organic Business

Combined Business
