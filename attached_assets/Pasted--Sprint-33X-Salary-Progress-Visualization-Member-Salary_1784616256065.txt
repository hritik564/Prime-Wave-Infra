# Sprint 33X – Salary Progress & Visualization (Member Salary Center)

This sprint focuses entirely on the member experience.

No compensation rules shall be changed.

No business calculations shall be modified.

No qualification logic shall be altered.

No payout logic shall be changed.

The backend already contains all required business data.

This sprint exposes that information in a beautiful, motivational and easy-to-understand experience for every PrimeWave member.

--------------------------------------------------
OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------

Transform the existing Salary page into a complete Salary Center where members can clearly understand:

• Their current salary position

• Their qualification progress

• Locked business

• Unlocked business

• Salary history

• Upcoming salary

• What is required to reach the next salary rank

Everything displayed must come directly from the canonical backend services.

No duplicated calculations.
