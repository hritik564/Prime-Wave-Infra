## DESIGN GOAL

When users open this page they should immediately understand:

• How healthy their network is.

• Which leg needs attention.

• Where the next Binary reward is likely to come from.

• Which members deserve follow-up.

• Why they earned their last reward.

The React Flow tree should become the confirmation tool, while the intelligence layer becomes the decision-making tool.

This page should feel like a Network Intelligence Command Center, not just a binary tree viewer.
