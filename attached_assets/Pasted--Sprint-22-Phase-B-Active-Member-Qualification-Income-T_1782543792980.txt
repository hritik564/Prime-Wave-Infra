# Sprint 22 — Phase B: Active Member Qualification & Income Transfer Engine

Objective

This sprint focuses on two core systems:

1. Active Member Qualification Engine
2. Income Wallet → Withdrawal Wallet 72-Hour Transfer Engine

Do NOT redesign the UI.

Do NOT modify investments.

Do NOT modify Binary calculations.

Do NOT modify ROI calculations.

Only implement and enforce the business rules below.

--------------------------------------------------------
PART 1 — Active Member Qualification Engine
--------------------------------------------------------

Prime Wave Business Rule (LOCKED)

A member is considered ACTIVE only if BOTH conditions are satisfied.

Condition 1

The member has at least:

$100 Active Investment

AND

Condition 2

The member has:

• Minimum 1 Direct Referral on the LEFT leg

AND

• Minimum 1 Direct Referral on the RIGHT leg

Both conditions are mandatory.

--------------------------------------------------------

Inactive Members

Inactive members are eligible ONLY for:

✓ ROI Income

✓ Principal Return

Inactive members are NOT eligible for:

✗ Binary Income

✗ Binary Orbit Income

✗ Salary Income

✗ Level Income

These income engines must completely ignore inactive members.

--------------------------------------------------------

Implementation

Create one centralized eligibility service.

Example:

isMemberActive(userId)

Do NOT duplicate qualification logic inside Binary, Salary, Orbit and Level engines.

Every income engine must call the centralized eligibility service.

--------------------------------------------------------

Active Status

Whenever any of the following changes:

• Investment

• Sponsor Tree

• Left Referral

• Right Referral

Automatically recalculate member status.

No manual refresh should be required.

--------------------------------------------------------

User Dashboard

Display member status.

ACTIVE

or

INACTIVE

If inactive, show:

Requirements Remaining

Examples:

✓ Minimum Investment Completed

✓ Left Referral Completed

❌ Right Referral Required

OR

❌ Minimum $100 Investment Required

✓ Left Referral Completed

✓ Right Referral Completed

This should update automatically.
