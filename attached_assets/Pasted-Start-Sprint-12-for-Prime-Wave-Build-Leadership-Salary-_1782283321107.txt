========================================
QUALIFICATION RULES
========================================

Bronze, Silver, Gold:

- Direct business only.
- Single leg allowed.
- Spill business ignored.
- No direct requirement.

Platinum and above:

Require:

1 personally sponsored member placed LEFT.
1 personally sponsored member placed RIGHT.

Not just two directs anywhere.

Spill business counts.

Power leg and weaker leg requirements must be validated.

========================================
STACKING RULE
========================================

Salaries stack.

Example:

Bronze = $40
Silver = $75
Gold = $125

Monthly payout = $240

Each achievement expires independently.

========================================
MONTHLY CRON
========================================

Run on:

1st day of month
Europe/London timezone

Process:

1. Find active SalaryAchievements.
2. Credit Withdrawal Wallet.
3. Create WalletTransaction.
4. Create SalaryIncome record.
5. Automatically deactivate expired achievements.
