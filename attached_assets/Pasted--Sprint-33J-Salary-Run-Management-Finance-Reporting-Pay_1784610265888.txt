# Sprint 33J – Salary Run Management, Finance Reporting & Payroll Audit

Sprint 33I has been approved.

The Salary Engine is now functionally complete.

This sprint introduces the Finance Operations layer.

⚠️ IMPORTANT

This sprint MUST NOT modify:

- Qualification
- SalaryBusinessSummary
- SalaryBusinessEvent
- SalaryAchievement
- Salary Scheduler
- Monthly Snapshot Engine
- Salary Payout rules

Those domains are complete.

This sprint focuses entirely on payroll management, finance reporting, auditing, and operational visibility.
