SPRINT 27 — DEPOSIT OPERATIONS & VISIBILITY

Objective

Transform the Deposit Verification system into a complete operational workflow.

Every submitted transaction must always be visible.

Users should always know where their money is.

Admins should always have recovery tools.

This sprint is presentation + operational tooling.

Do NOT modify:

- Blockchain verification logic
- BSCBlockchainAdapter
- Wallet credit calculations
- approveDeposit()
- Financial calculations
- Investment Engine
- ROI Engine
- Binary Engine
- Salary Engine
- Orbit Engine
- Level Income Engine

Reuse existing production business logic.

====================================================
PART 1 — DEPOSIT HISTORY BECOMES SOURCE OF TRUTH
====================================================

Every submitted TX hash must immediately appear in Deposit History.

Do NOT wait until the deposit is credited.

Create/update history entries during the entire verification lifecycle.

Supported statuses:

• Submitted
• Verifying
• Waiting for Confirmations
• Verification Delayed
• Completed
• Failed
• Duplicate

The user must never wonder whether the system received the transaction.
