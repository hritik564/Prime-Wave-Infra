SPRINT 26A — INCOME CENTER REDESIGN (OVERVIEW)

Objective

Redesign the Income Center into an investor-first dashboard.

This sprint is NOT a financial engine sprint.

It is a presentation-layer sprint only.

The existing ROI, Binary, Orbit, Salary, Level Income, Wallet, Investment, Scheduler, and Transparency engines are the single source of truth.

Do NOT duplicate or reimplement business logic.

Do NOT modify any payout engine.

Do NOT change any database schema.

Only aggregate existing data and present it more clearly.

------------------------------------------------------------

PRIMARY GOAL

When a member opens Income Center they should instantly know:

1. How much have I earned?
2. What is my next payout?
3. How much is expected next?
4. Am I qualified?
5. What should I do to earn more?

Everything else becomes secondary.

------------------------------------------------------------

REMOVE FROM INCOME CENTER

The current page is overloaded.

Remove from the overview:

• Portfolio Mix chart
• Earnings Trend graph
• Analytics cards
• Technical percentages
• Engine implementation details
• Transfer engine information
• Infrastructure status

These belong on future analytics/detail pages.
