Business Rules (LOCKED)

1. ROI is generated ONLY from active investments.

2. ROI is credited ONLY to the Income Wallet.

3. Principal (Capital Return) is credited ONLY to the Income Wallet.

4. Binary Income is generated ONLY from matched Left BV and Right BV and is credited directly to the Withdrawal Wallet.

5. Level Income is credited directly to the Withdrawal Wallet.

6. Salary Income is credited directly to the Withdrawal Wallet.

7. Binary Orbit Income is credited directly to the Withdrawal Wallet.

8. Income generated by the system is immutable and cannot be manually edited.

9. The Income Engine is responsible only for calculating and recording income. It must not modify wallet balances outside the defined business rules.
