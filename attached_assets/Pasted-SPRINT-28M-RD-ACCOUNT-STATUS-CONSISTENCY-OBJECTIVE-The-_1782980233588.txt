SPRINT 28M — RD ACCOUNT STATUS CONSISTENCY

OBJECTIVE

The production investigation is complete.

Root cause has been proven:

- All 64 RD accounts have one ACTIVE investment.
- All 64 RD accounts remain users.status = INACTIVE.
- The Admin UI is correct.
- The Dashboard is correct.
- The inconsistency exists only because rdActivation.ts never updated users.status.

This sprint fixes that inconsistency.

==========================================================
BUSINESS RULE
==========================================================

A genesis placeholder account (users.status = INACTIVE) becomes a live platform member immediately after its FIRST successful qualifying investment.

For Sprint 28M we are applying this ONLY to the RD activation flow.

No other business behaviour changes.

- Update users.status = ACTIVE

Requirements:

- Only update if current status == INACTIVE.
- Do not overwrite SUSPENDED.
- Do not overwrite BLOCKED.
- Do not perform the update if investment creation failed.
- The status update must happen only after successful investment creation.

IDEMPOTENCY
==========================================================

The script must remain completely idempotent.

- Not create duplicate deposits.
- Not create duplicate investments.
- Not rewrite ACTIVE users unnecessarily.
- Produce zero side effects.
