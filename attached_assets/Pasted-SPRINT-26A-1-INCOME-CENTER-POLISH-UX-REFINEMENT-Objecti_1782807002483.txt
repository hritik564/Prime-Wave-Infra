====================================================
1. RENAME SNAPSHOT CARD
====================================================

Replace

"Pending This Cycle"

with

"Expected Next Credits"

Reason:

"Pending" sounds like approval is waiting.

The card actually represents expected upcoming income.

The description should read:

"Expected income from your upcoming payout cycle."

If no estimate exists:

Display

"Calculating..."

Never display

$0

unless the engine has positively determined the payout is zero.
