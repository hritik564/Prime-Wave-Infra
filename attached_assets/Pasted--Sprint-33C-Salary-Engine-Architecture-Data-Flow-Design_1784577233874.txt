# Sprint 33C – Salary Engine Architecture & Data Flow (Design Only)

Sprint 33A and Sprint 33B have been approved.

DO NOT write implementation code in this sprint.

This is an architecture and design sprint only.

We are redesigning the Salary Qualification engine before any production code is changed.

The objective is to ensure the compensation plan is clean, transparent, scalable and completely separated from the Binary engine.

--------------------------------------------------
IMPORTANT BUSINESS RULES
--------------------------------------------------

Salary is NOT Binary.

Salary should become its own independent qualification system.

Binary Matching and Binary Orbit are already completed.

Salary should no longer rely on Binary BV logic.

--------------------------------------------------
NEW SALARY MODEL
--------------------------------------------------

We now have three independent business pools.

POOL 1

Direct Business

Definition:

Business generated ONLY from personally sponsored (Level 1) members.

Purpose:

Qualification for:

Bronze

Silver

Gold

Only Direct Business should count for these three ranks.

--------------------------------------------------

POOL 2

Team Business

Definition:

Business generated by the entire downline below personally sponsored members.

Characteristics:

• Automatically accumulated

• Visible to the member

• Locked

• Cannot be used until Gold Salary Rank is achieved

After Gold is achieved,

Team Business automatically unlocks.

--------------------------------------------------

POOL 3

Power Business

Definition:

Business granted manually through the new Power BV system.

Characteristics:

• Automatically accumulated

• Visible

• Locked

• Unlocks only after Gold Salary Rank

Power Business follows exactly the same unlock rules as Team Business.

--------------------------------------------------
UI REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------

The Salary dashboard should eventually show:

Current Rank

Direct Business Progress

Locked Team Business

Locked Power Business

Example message:

"Achieve Gold Salary Rank to unlock your Team Business and Power Business for higher salary qualifications."

Nothing should ever appear hidden.

Everything should remain transparent.
