# Sprint 33Z – Remove Historical Seeded Business & Canonicalize Power Business

This sprint removes the historical demo/read-only seeded business volume that was introduced for the early network demonstration.

From this point forward, manually awarded business must ONLY originate from the Admin Power Business system.

No fake deposits.

No seeded placeholder investments.

No hidden reporting-only business.

--------------------------------------------------
OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------

Remove all historical demo seed business from production.

Replace its purpose with the existing Admin Power Business system.

--------------------------------------------------
BUSINESS RULE
--------------------------------------------------

Admin Power Business represents manually granted business volume.

It SHALL:

✓ Contribute to Binary Matching.

✓ Contribute to Binary Orbit.

✓ Contribute to Salary qualification (when eligible).

✓ Appear in Network Intelligence.

✓ Appear in Salary Center.

✓ Be fully auditable.

It SHALL NOT:

✗ Generate Direct Referral Income.

✗ Create fake deposits.

✗ Create fake investments.

✗ Create wallet transactions pretending to be deposits.

--------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 – IDENTIFY DEMO SEEDS
--------------------------------------------------

Locate every deposit/account created solely for the historical demo.

Produce a report including:

- User
- Deposit ID
- Amount
- Approval date
- Binary placement
- Reason classified as demo seed

--------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 – REMOVE DEMO SEEDS
--------------------------------------------------

Delete or safely reverse every demo seed.

Remove any derived reporting data generated solely from those demo seeds.

Preserve genuine member deposits.

--------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 – RECALCULATE
--------------------------------------------------

Rebuild:

- Binary Team Business
- Left Business
- Right Business
- Matching availability
- Orbit qualification

using only genuine production activity plus Power Business.

--------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 – POWER BUSINESS VERIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------

Verify that Power Business now participates in:

✓ Binary Matching

✓ Binary Orbit

✓ Salary qualification

and does NOT generate:

✗ Direct Referral Income

--------------------------------------------------
STEP 5 – AUDIT
--------------------------------------------------

Confirm that every manually awarded business can be traced to an Admin Power Business record.

There should be no remaining hidden or seeded business.

--------------------------------------------------
DELIVERABLES
--------------------------------------------------

Return:

1. Demo seed accounts identified.

2. Demo seed deposits removed.

3. Binary totals before/after.

4. Matching totals before/after.

5. Orbit totals before/after.

6. Salary impact before/after.

7. Verification that Direct Income is unaffected.

8. Final PASS/FAIL.

--------------------------------------------------
SUCCESS CRITERIA
--------------------------------------------------

PASS only if:

✓ No historical demo seed business remains.

✓ Network Intelligence reflects only genuine deposits plus Admin Power Business.

✓ Binary Matching recognises Admin Power Business.

✓ Binary Orbit recognises Admin Power Business.

✓ Salary engine recognises Admin Power Business.

✓ Direct Income ignores Admin Power Business.

✓ Every manual business adjustment is auditable through the Admin Power Business system.
