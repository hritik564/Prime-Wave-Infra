# Sprint QA-4 — Binary Compensation Rule Update & Transparency

## Objective

Update the Binary Matching and Binary Orbit engines to match the finalized Prime Wave compensation plan.

In addition, improve transparency by recording every payout cycle in the ledger, history pages and notifications.

Do NOT change the payout amounts.

Only change the BV processing rules and audit trail.

---

# 1. Binary Matching (Final Business Rule)

Every 12 hours:

Calculate:

Matched BV = min(Left BV, Right BV)

Maximum payable BV per cycle:

50 BV

Reward:

Rewarded BV = min(Matched BV, 50)

Binary Income = Rewarded BV × $5

Maximum payout:

$250

### IMPORTANT

ALL matched BV must be flushed.

Not only the rewarded BV.

Only unmatched BV carries forward.

Example:

Left = 60 BV

Right = 100 BV

Matched = 60 BV

Rewarded BV = 50 BV

Income = $250

Flushed BV = 60 BV

Carry Forward:

Left = 0 BV

Right = 40 BV

---

Example:

Left = 200 BV

Right = 200 BV

Matched = 200 BV

Rewarded BV = 50 BV

Income = $250

Flushed BV = 200 BV

Carry Forward:

Left = 0

Right = 0

# 2. Binary Orbit (Final Business Rule)

Every 12 hours:

Matched BV = min(Left BV, Right BV)

Qualification:

Matched BV must be at least 150.

Reward:

$500

Maximum one reward per cycle.

### IMPORTANT

ALL matched BV must be flushed.

Only unmatched BV carries forward.
