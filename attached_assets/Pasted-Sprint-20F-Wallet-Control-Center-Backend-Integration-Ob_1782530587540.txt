Business Rules (LOCKED)

Prime Wave has three wallets.

1. Deposit Wallet

• Receives user deposits.

• Used only to purchase investment plans.

2. Income Wallet

Receives ONLY:

• ROI

• Principal (Capital Return)

Users can transfer funds from the Income Wallet.

When a transfer is initiated:

• No admin approval is required.

• The transfer automatically enters the 72-hour release engine.

• The transfer is released automatically in 72 equal hourly releases.

• Released funds are credited to the Withdrawal Wallet.

3. Withdrawal Wallet

Receives:

• Binary Income

• Binary Orbit Income

• Level Income

• Salary Income

• Released transfers arriving from the Income Wallet every hour through the 72-hour release engine.

Users request withdrawals ONLY from the Withdrawal Wallet.

Withdrawal requests require manual admin approval and are handled by the Withdrawal Center.

The Wallet Control Center is a monitoring and auditing module.

It must NEVER manually trigger wallet movements or bypass business rules.
