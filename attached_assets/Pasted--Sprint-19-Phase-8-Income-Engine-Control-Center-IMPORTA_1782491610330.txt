# Sprint 19 — Phase 8

## Income Engine Control Center

IMPORTANT

This is the administrative command center for the Prime Wave compensation engine.

It controls and monitors every income source on the platform.

This is NOT a finance dashboard.

This is the operational engine used to monitor, audit and manage all income generation.

Use realistic mock data only.

Do NOT connect backend.

Do NOT modify existing modules.

Continue using the Prime Wave Admin design language.
