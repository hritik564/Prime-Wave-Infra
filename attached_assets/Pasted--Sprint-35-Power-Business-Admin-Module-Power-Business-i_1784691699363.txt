# Sprint 35 — Power Business Admin Module

Power Business is now a core component of the PrimeWave compensation engine.

It deserves its own dedicated module in the Admin Control Center.

This sprint creates a standalone Power Business section.

Do NOT change any compensation logic.

This is an admin management module only.

--------------------------------------------------
SIDEBAR
--------------------------------------------------

Add a new top-level navigation item:

⚡ Power Business

Place it under:

Income Engine

or

Binary Network

(use the position that best fits the current sidebar layout).

--------------------------------------------------
PAGE 1 — Dashboard
--------------------------------------------------

Create a landing page for Power Business.

Display:

• Total Active Power BV

• Total Power Business ($ equivalent)

• Total Members with Power BV

• Total Power BV Assigned

• Total Power BV Consumed

• Remaining Active Power BV

• Today's Assignments

• Last 7 Days Assignments

--------------------------------------------------
PAGE 2 — Search Member
--------------------------------------------------

Search by:

• Username

• User ID

• Name

Display:

Current Power BV

Left Power BV

Right Power BV

Power Business ($)

Organic Team Business

Total Team Business

Quick link to Member Profile

--------------------------------------------------
PAGE 3 — Assign Power BV
--------------------------------------------------

Move the existing Power BV assignment functionality into this module.

Support:

• Left Power BV

• Right Power BV

• Reason (required)

• Notes (optional)

• Preview before assignment

Show equivalent business value:

Example:

185 BV

=

$18,500 Team Business
