SPRINT 26C — INCOME INSIGHTS & NEXT ACTIONS

Objective

Transform every income detail page from a reporting page into an intelligent coaching page.

The platform should explain:

• WHY the current payout is what it is
• WHY the user is (or isn't) qualified
• WHAT the user should do next
• WHAT action gives the biggest increase in income

This sprint is presentation and aggregation only.

Do NOT modify:

- ROI Engine
- Binary Engine
- Orbit Engine
- Salary Engine
- Level Income Engine
- Wallet Engine
- Investment Engine
- Deposit Verification
- Withdrawal
- Scheduler
- Database schema
- Financial calculations

====================================================
NEW SECTION

"YOUR NEXT BEST ACTION"

Every detail page gets a new card directly below the Hero.

This card must always answer:

"What should I do next?"

Never show generic advice.

Advice must come from actual production data.

"Your fastest way to increase ROI is increasing your principal investment."

"Auto Compound is currently OFF.
Enable it if you want future ROI reinvested automatically."
