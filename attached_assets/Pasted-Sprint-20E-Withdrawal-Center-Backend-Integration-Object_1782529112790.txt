Objective

IMPORTANT

--------------------------------------------------

Business Rules (Locked)

Withdrawal requests always originate from the user's Withdrawal Wallet.

Every withdrawal request requires manual approval by an authorized administrator.

The 72-hour release engine DOES NOT apply to withdrawals.

The 72-hour release engine is used ONLY for Income Wallet transfers and is handled automatically outside this module.
