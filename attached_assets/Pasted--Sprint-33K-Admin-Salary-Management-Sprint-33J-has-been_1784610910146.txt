# Sprint 33K – Admin Salary Management

Sprint 33J has been approved.

This is the final sprint of the Salary module.

The Salary Engine is complete.

This sprint adds a lightweight Admin interface for operating the monthly salary process.

⚠️ IMPORTANT

This sprint MUST NOT modify any salary business logic.

Do NOT change:

- Salary Qualification
- Salary Scheduler
- Salary Generation
- Salary Approval
- Salary Payout
- SalaryRun
- Wallet logic
- Business rules

This sprint is UI and operational management only.

--------------------------------------------------
OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------

Build a simple Admin Salary Management page.

The page should allow administrators to:

• View Salary Runs

• View Monthly Payroll

• Review Payroll Status

• Approve Payroll

• Execute Payroll

• Retry failed member payouts

• View Member Salary History

• Download payroll CSV

No new business logic.

Only consume existing backend services.
