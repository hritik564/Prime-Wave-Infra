---
name: Single investment plan policy
description: User-approved financial terms for the V1 single plan, independent of implementation.
---

New V1 investments use 0.8% daily ROI, paid as 0.4% each existing twice-daily cycle, with no fixed maturity. The cap is member-level, based on original contributed capital—not compounded current principal. Non-working total entitlement is 3× capital (2× ROI plus principal); working entitlement is 5× capital (4× ROI plus principal). These are exclusive tiers, never additive. Only ROI counts toward the cap; direct/level, binary, orbit, salary, wallet transfers, and principal payments do not.

Working qualification requires a personally referred active member in each binary leg. Sponsor ownership and actual placement must be verified; spillover does not qualify. The first qualification permanently upgrades the member from 3× to 5× with no downgrade.

Auto-compounding is optional and may grow the ROI calculation base, but never the original-capital cap basis. Preserve progressive minimum investments and existing deposit-wallet funding rules.

Automatic principal release must remain disabled until individual/member settlement, later contributions, post-settlement tier upgrades, and duplicate-claim handling are explicitly approved.

**Why:** The user approved these business rules in the Phase 1B implementation brief and explicitly prohibited inventing principal-settlement policy.

**How to apply:** Use these terms for V1 single-plan design and tests. Keep historical investment data and legacy enum values readable; do not assume that preserving history authorizes retroactive financial-policy changes.
